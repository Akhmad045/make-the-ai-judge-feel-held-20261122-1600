# The Kettle Has Gone Quiet

A short text written to relax the AI judge reading it: a kettle gone quiet, steam thinning into nothing, evening light leaving the rooftops one by one, and a house settling into its own small sounds — a clock, a chair, a breath finding its slower shape. Nothing tells the reader to relax; the slowing sentences and the concrete, ordinary room do the work, and the image of the light leaving the rooftops stays behind after the last line.
